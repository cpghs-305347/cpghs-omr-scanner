📘 User Instructions — How to Set Up Keys & Google Drive Link (Click to Collapse/Expand)
  1. Step 1 (Keys and TOS Tab): Tap "🔄 Sync Students & Keys" below. Select the Subject and Test Type to generate the Exam ID. Enter the Answer Key, map your TOS (Learning Competencies & Item Numbers), and paste your target Google Drive Folder Link (e.g. https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/...), then tap "💾 Save Key & TOS to Sheet".
  2. Step 2 (Image Capture Tab): Switch to Image Capture, verify that the Drive Repository banner displays your updated Google Drive link, and tap "📸 Take Photo / HDR & Grade". Each graded sheet uploads directly to your Google Drive folder automatically.
  3. Step 3 (Item Analytics Tab): Open Item Analytics to review Mean Percentage Score (MPS), per-item difficulty/discrimination, Top 10 Least Mastered Competencies, and Student Rankings.
