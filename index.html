📘 User Instructions — How to Set Up Keys & Capture Answer Sheets (Click to Collapse/Expand)
  1. Step 1 (Keys and TOS Tab): Click or tap "🔄 Sync Students & Keys" below. Next, select the Subject and Test Type to automatically generate the Exam ID. Fill out the Answer Key and TOS (Learning Competencies & Item Numbers). Next, create or set the Folder Path where you want to save the image captures, then click or tap "💾 Save Key & TOS to Sheet".
  2. Step 2 (Image Capture Tab): Go to the Image Capture tab, select your Saved Exam Key, and start capturing answer sheets by tapping "📸 Take Photo / HDR & Grade". After taking the photo, the Folder Path is automatically prefilled from your saved Exam ID setup—do not alter the image scanning settings.
  3. Step 3 (Item Analytics Tab): Open Item Analytics to review Mean Percentage Score (MPS), per-item difficulty/discrimination, Top 10 Least Mastered Competencies, and Student Rankings (recorded in Philippine Standard Time — PST).
